Daylight — Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 7, 2026

Daylight is a browser new-tab extension that provides a customizable dashboard with wallpapers, themes, weather information, website shortcuts, web search and Spotify playlist embedding.

We respect your privacy and aim to collect only the information necessary to provide these features.

1. Information Stored Locally

Daylight may store the following information on your device:

  • Your preferred display name.
  • Selected themes and custom accent colors.
  • Wallpaper images you upload.
  • Weather and dashboard preferences.
  • Spotify playlist preferences.

This information is stored locally in your browser using browser storage technologies such as Chrome Storage and IndexedDB. Daylight does not send these preferences to its own backend server.

2. Location Information and Weather

If you allow location access, Daylight may use your device's location to display local weather information.

Your location coordinates may be sent to the Open-Meteo service to retrieve weather information.

Location access is optional. You can deny browser location permission or disable the weather feature.

Open-Meteo privacy information: https://open-meteo.com/en/terms

3. Search Information

When you use the search box, your search query is sent to the search engine you select, such as Google or Bing.

The selected search provider processes that query according to its own privacy policy.

Daylight does not operate its own search service.

4. Spotify Integration

If you use the Spotify playlist feature, Daylight may display content through an embedded Spotify player.

Spotify may receive technical information such as your IP address and browser information when the embedded player loads.

Spotify processes information under its own privacy policy: https://www.spotify.com/legal/privacy-policy/

5. Data Sharing and Selling

Daylight does not sell users' personal information.

Daylight does not operate a backend server that collects or stores user preferences.

Information may be transmitted to third-party services when you actively use features such as weather, web search or the Spotify embed. Those services process information under their own privacy policies.

6. Data Security and Control

Your locally stored preferences and uploaded wallpapers can be cleared by removing the relevant extension data or uninstalling the extension.

You can control location access through your browser's site and extension permissions.

7. Children's Privacy

Daylight is a general-purpose browser customization extension and is not specifically designed to collect personal information from children.

8. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when Daylight's features or data practices change. Updates will be published on this page.
